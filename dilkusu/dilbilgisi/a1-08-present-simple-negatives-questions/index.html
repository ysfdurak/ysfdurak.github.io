Present simple: negatives & questions

Geniş zaman: olumsuz ve soru · A1 Başlangıç seviye İngilizce dilbilgisi

Olumsuz: don't / doesn't

Present simple'da olumsuz cümle kurmak için fiilden önce don't (do not) ya da doesn't (does not) kullanılır. doesn't kullanınca fiil yalın hâle döner, -s düşer.

ÖzneOlumsuz
I / you / we / theyI don't like fish.
he / she / itShe doesn't like fish.

Soru: Do / Does

SoruKısa cevap
Do you work here?Yes, I do. / No, I don't.
Does he speak English?Yes, he does. / No, he doesn't.
Where do they live?They live in Ankara.
What does she do?She's a nurse.
  • I don't eat meat.Et yemem.
  • He doesn't work on Fridays.Cuma günleri çalışmaz.
  • Do you like tea?Çay sever misin?
  • What time does the film start?Film saat kaçta başlıyor?
Türkçeden farkı: Türkçede soru "-mı" ekiyle yapılır: "Çay sever misin?" İngilizcede cümlenin başına Do / Does gelir: "Do you like tea?" Does kullandığında fiile -s eklenmez: "Does she like tea?" ("Does she likes" yanlış).

Sık yapılan hatalar

YanlışDoğruNeden
She doesn't likes coffee.She doesn't like coffee.doesn't sonrası yalın fiil.
You like pizza?Do you like pizza?Soru için Do gerekir.
Where he lives?Where does he live?Soru kelimesinden sonra does.

Kendini dene

___ they live near here?

Seçenekler: Do / Does

Cevap: Do. they ile Do.

I ___ like horror films.

Seçenekler: don't / doesn't / not

Cevap: don't. I ile don't.

She ___ eat breakfast.

Seçenekler: don't / doesn't

Cevap: doesn't. she ile doesn't.

___ your brother play tennis?

Seçenekler: Do / Does

Cevap: Does. he ile Does.

Does he live here? — No, he ___.

Seçenekler: don't / doesn't / isn't

Cevap: doesn't. Kısa cevap: No, he doesn't.
