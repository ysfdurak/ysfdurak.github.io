Future in the past

Geçmişten bakılan gelecek · B2 Orta seviye İngilizce dilbilgisi

Geçmişten bakılan gelecek

Geçmişteki bir andan bakıldığında "gelecek" olan şeyleri anlatmak için gelecek zaman yapıları geçmişe kaydırılır.

Şimdiden bakışGeçmişten bakışÖrnek
willwouldI knew she would be late.
be going towas / were going toWe were going to visit you, but we got ill.
present continuous (plan)past continuousI was meeting Ali that evening, so I left early.
be about towas / were about toI was about to leave when the phone rang.

Gerçekleşmeyen planlar

was / were going to genellikle gerçekleşmeyen bir planı anlatır: I was going to call you, but I forgot. (Seni arayacaktım ama unuttum.) Aynı anlamda: was / were supposed to (-mesi gerekiyordu), was / were to have + V3 (resmî).

Türkçeden farkı: Türkçede "-ecekti / -acaktı" (gelecekti, arayacaktım) bu yapıların karşılığıdır. "Arayacaktım ama..." cümlesi neredeyse her zaman "I was going to call you, but..." ile çevrilir. Dolaylı anlatımda will'in would'a dönüşmesi de aynı mantıktır.

Sık yapılan hatalar

YanlışDoğruNeden
I knew he will come.I knew he would come.Geçmişten bakış: would.
I am going to call you, but I forgot.I was going to call you, but I forgot.Geçmiş plan: was going to.
She was about leaving.She was about to leave.about to + fiil.

Kendini dene

I was sure he ___ pass the exam.

Seçenekler: will / would

Cevap: would. Geçmişten bakış: would.

We ___ to arrive at 6, but the train was delayed.

Seçenekler: were supposed / would / are going

Cevap: were supposed. were supposed to.

I ___ going to buy that dress, but it was sold out.

Seçenekler: was / am / would

Cevap: was. Gerçekleşmeyen plan.

She promised she ___ help me.

Seçenekler: will / would / is going to

Cevap: would. would.

We were ___ to start when the lights went out.

Seçenekler: about / going / supposed

Cevap: about. about to.
