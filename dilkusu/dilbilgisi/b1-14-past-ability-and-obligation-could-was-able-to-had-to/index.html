Past ability and obligation: could, was able to, had to

Geçmişte yetenek ve zorunluluk · B1 Orta öncesi seviye İngilizce dilbilgisi

Geçmişte yetenek: could ve was able to

Geçmişteki genel bir yeteneği anlatmak için could kullanılır: I could swim when I was five. Ama tek bir durumda zorlukla başarılan bir şeyi anlatırken could değil, was / were able to (ya da managed to) kullanılır.

DurumDoğru yapıÖrnek
genel yetenekcould / was able toShe could speak three languages.
tek seferlik başarıwas able to / managed toThe fire was big, but everyone was able to escape.
olumsuz (her ikisi)couldn't / wasn't able toI couldn't find my keys.

Geçmişte zorunluluk: had to

must'ın geçmişi yoktur; geçmişteki zorunluluk had to ile anlatılır: I had to work late yesterday. Olumsuz: didn't have to (gerek yoktu): We didn't have to pay. Soru: Did you have to wait?

will be able to

can'ın gelecek ve perfect biçimi yoktur; bunun yerine be able to: I'll be able to help you tomorrow. I haven't been able to sleep.

Türkçeden farkı: Türkçede "yapabildim" her durumda kullanılır. İngilizcede tek bir başarı için "I could" doğal değildir; "I was able to / I managed to" denir. Olumsuzda ise fark yoktur: "I couldn't" her zaman doğrudur.
