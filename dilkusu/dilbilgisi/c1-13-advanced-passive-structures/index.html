İleri edilgen yapılar

B1 ve B2'de temel edilgen ve edilgen aktarma yapılarını gördük. C1'de daha karmaşık edilgen kullanımlara bakıyoruz.

YapıÖrnek
iki nesneli fiillerI was given a present. / A present was given to me.
edilgen + mastarHe was made to wait. She was seen to leave.
get edilgenHe got fired. My car got stolen. (gündelik, genelde olumsuz olay)
edilgen -ingI don't like being lied to.
edilgen + edatShe was laughed at. The issue has been dealt with.
need / want + -ingThe car needs servicing.

Algı ve ettirgen fiiller

make, see, hear etken yapıda yalın fiil alır, edilgen yapıda to alır: They made him wait. → He was made to wait. Someone saw her enter the building. → She was seen to enter the building.

Edilgen yapıyı ne zaman kullanmalı?

Eylemi yapanın bilinmediği, önemsiz olduğu ya da açık olduğu durumlarda; resmî ve bilimsel yazıda; ve cümlenin konusunu (teması) başta tutmak için. Ama her şeyi edilgen yapmak yazıyı ağırlaştırır.

Türkçeden farkı: Türkçede edilgen yapı çok doğaldır ve "-ıl, -ın" ekleriyle kolayca kurulur. İngilizcede de yaygındır, ama iki nesneli fiillerde kişi özne yapılır: "Bana bir hediye verildi" = I was given a present (doğal), A present was given to me (daha az doğal). Edatlı fiillerde edat kaybolmaz: "Bununla ilgilenildi" = It has been dealt with.

Sık yapılan hatalar

YanlışDoğruNeden
He was made wait.He was made to wait.Edilgende to.
She was laughed.She was laughed at.Edat kalır.
To me was given a book.I was given a book.Kişi özne.

Kendini dene

I ___ a free ticket by the manager.

Seçenekler: was given / gave / was give

Cevap: was given. İki nesneli: I was given.

She was seen ___ the building at midnight.

Seçenekler: leave / to leave / left

Cevap: to leave. Edilgen algı: to.

The children ___ by their grandmother.

Seçenekler: were looked after / were looked / looked after

Cevap: were looked after. Edat kalır.

He ___ arrested for drunk driving.

Seçenekler: got / had / made

Cevap: got. get edilgen.

We were made ___ for two hours.

Seçenekler: wait / to wait / waiting

Cevap: to wait. Edilgen: to.
