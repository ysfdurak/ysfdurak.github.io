Past simple vs past continuous: when / while

Geçmiş zamanlar: when / while · A2 Temel seviye İngilizce dilbilgisi

Uzun eylem ve kısa eylem

Geçmişte süren uzun bir eylemin (past continuous) ortasında kısa bir eylem (past simple) olduğunda iki zaman birlikte kullanılır.

  • I was walking home when I saw an accident.Eve yürürken bir kaza gördüm.
  • When the phone rang, she was having a bath.Telefon çaldığında banyo yapıyordu.

when ve while

BağlaçNe ile?Örnek
whengenellikle kısa eylem (past simple)I was cooking when he arrived.
whilegenellikle uzun eylem (past continuous)While I was cooking, he arrived.
while + whileaynı anda süren iki eylemI was reading while she was sleeping.

Art arda olaylar

Olaylar sırayla olduysa ikisi de past simple olur: When he arrived, we had dinner. (Önce geldi, sonra yedik.) Karşılaştırın: When he arrived, we were having dinner. (Geldiğinde zaten yiyorduk.)

Türkçeden farkı: Türkçede "-ırken" eki while'a, "-dığında" eki when'e karşılık gelir: "yürürken" = while I was walking, "geldiğinde" = when he came. when cümlesi başa gelirse arkasına virgül konur.

Sık yapılan hatalar

YanlışDoğruNeden
While I walked, I was seeing him.While I was walking, I saw him.Uzun: continuous, kısa: simple.
When I was arriving, they left.When I arrived, they were leaving. / When I arrived, they had left.Kısa eylem: arrived.
I was cooking while he arrived.I was cooking when he arrived.Kısa eylem: when.

Kendini dene

I ___ TV when the lights went out.

Seçenekler: watched / was watching

Cevap: was watching. Uzun eylem: was watching.

We were sleeping when the alarm ___.

Seçenekler: went off / was going off

Cevap: went off. Kısa eylem: went off.

"When she came in, everybody stood up." Ne oldu?

Seçenekler: Herkes zaten ayaktaydı. / O girince herkes ayağa kalktı.

Cevap: O girince herkes ayağa kalktı.. İki past simple: sırayla.

She ___ her leg while she was skiing.

Seçenekler: broke / was breaking

Cevap: broke. Kısa eylem: broke.

What were you doing when I ___?

Seçenekler: called / was calling

Cevap: called. Kısa eylem: called.
