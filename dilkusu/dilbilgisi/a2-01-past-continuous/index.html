Past continuous

Geçmişte süregelen zaman · A2 Temel seviye İngilizce dilbilgisi

Geçmişte süren eylem

Past continuous, geçmişte belirli bir anda devam etmekte olan eylemi anlatır: "Dün saat sekizde yemek yiyordum." Yapısı: was / were + fiil-ing.

ÖzneOlumluOlumsuzSoru
I / he / she / itwas workingwasn't workingWas she working?
you / we / theywere workingweren't workingWere they working?
  • At 8 o'clock last night I was having dinner.Dün akşam saat sekizde yemek yiyordum.
  • What were you doing at midnight?Gece yarısı ne yapıyordun?
  • It was raining all morning.Bütün sabah yağmur yağıyordu.

Arka plan anlatmak

Hikâye anlatırken sahneyi kurmak için past continuous kullanılır: "The sun was shining and birds were singing." Olaylar ise past simple ile sıralanır.

Türkçeden farkı: Türkçedeki "-iyordu" eki büyük ölçüde past continuous'a karşılık gelir: yapıyordum = I was doing. Ama Türkçede "biliyordum, istiyordum" dediğimiz durum fiilleri İngilizcede past simple olur: I knew, I wanted.

Kendini dene

At ten o'clock yesterday I ___ TV.

Seçenekler: watched / was watching / were watching

Cevap: was watching. Belirli anda süren: was watching.

What ___ you doing at 9 p.m.?

Seçenekler: was / were / did

Cevap: were. you: were.

He ___ a shower when the phone rang.

Seçenekler: had / was having / has

Cevap: was having. Süren eylem: was having.

The children ___ sleeping, they were playing.

Seçenekler: wasn't / weren't / didn't

Cevap: weren't. they: weren't.

I ___ him very well.

Seçenekler: was knowing / knew

Cevap: knew. Durum fiili: knew.
