İleri karşılaştırma yapıları

B1 ve B2'de temel karşılaştırma, as ... as, the more ... the more yapılarını gördük. C1'de daha ince ve resmî karşılaştırma araçlarına bakıyoruz.

YapıAnlamÖrnek
twice / three times as ... asiki / üç katThe new model is twice as fast as the old one.
nowhere near as / nothing like ashiç o kadar değilIt's nowhere near as hot as last year.
by far / easily + superlativeaçık farklaShe is by far the best student.
the + comparative of the twoikisinden daha ... olanıHe is the taller of the two brothers.
no / not any + comparativehiç de daha ... değilThis one is no better than that one.
as many / much as + sayı... kadar çok (vurgu)As many as 500 people were injured.
every bit as ... astamamen aynı derecedeShe is every bit as talented as her sister.

Karşılaştırmada devrik yapı ve zamirler

Resmî yazıda than ve as'ten sonra devrik yapı görülebilir: The north is wealthier than is the south. Zamir seçimi: Günlük dilde "taller than me", resmî dilde "taller than I (am)".

Türkçeden farkı: Türkçede "iki kat daha hızlı" deriz; İngilizcede bunun en doğal karşılığı "twice as fast as"tır ("two times faster" de duyulur ama daha az standarttır). "Açık ara en iyi" = by far the best. "Hiç de daha iyi değil" = no better than. Bu kalıplar özellikle grafik ve veri anlatırken (IELTS Writing Task 1) çok kullanışlıdır.

Kendini dene

This exercise is ___ the most difficult in the book.

Seçenekler: by far / very / much more

Cevap: by far. by far + superlative.

Of the two candidates, she is ___.

Seçenekler: the most qualified / the more qualified / more qualified

Cevap: the more qualified. İkiden: the more.

The second half was ___ as exciting as the first.

Seçenekler: nowhere near / by far / no

Cevap: nowhere near. nowhere near.

This phone is ___ better than the old one. It has the same problems.

Seçenekler: no / nothing / any

Cevap: no. no better than.

___ as 10,000 fans attended the match.

Seçenekler: As many / As much / So many

Cevap: As many. As many as.
