by and with: means, method, agent, tool

by ve with · B1 Orta öncesi seviye İngilizce dilbilgisi

by: araç, yöntem ve fail

by + ulaşım aracı (artikelsiz): by car, by bus, by plane (ama on foot). by + yöntem: pay by card, send by email. by + -ing: You can improve by practising. Edilgen cümlede eylemi yapan: The book was written by Orhan Pamuk. Ayrıca by mistake (yanlışlıkla), by chance (tesadüfen).

KullanımÖrnek
UlaşımWe went by train.
YöntemCan I pay by credit card?
by + -ingHe lost weight by walking every day.
Edilgen failThe picture was painted by a child.
Kalıplarby mistake, by chance, by hand, by heart

with: alet ve birliktelik

with, bir eylemi yaparken kullanılan aleti anlatır: She cut the bread with a knife. Ayrıca birliktelik: I went with my friends. Karşılaştır: The window was broken by a boy (kim kırdı) / The window was broken with a stone (ne ile kırıldı).

Türkçeden farkı: Türkçede "-la / -le" eki hem araç (otobüsle) hem alet (bıçakla) hem birliktelik (arkadaşımla) için kullanılır. İngilizcede ulaşım ve yöntem by, alet ve birliktelik with ile anlatılır. "Arabamla geldim" = I came in my car (by car, araç belirli değilse).
