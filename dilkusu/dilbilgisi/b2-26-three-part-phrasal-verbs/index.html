Üç parçalı öbek fiiller

Bazı öbek fiiller bir fiil, bir zarf ve bir edattan oluşur: look forward to, get on with, put up with. Bu fiiller ayrılamaz; nesne en sona gelir.

Öbek fiilAnlamÖrnek
look forward todört gözle beklemekI'm looking forward to the holiday.
get on withiyi geçinmek; devam etmekI get on well with my boss. Let's get on with the work.
put up withkatlanmakI can't put up with this noise.
come up with(fikir) bulmakShe came up with a great idea.
run out oftükenmekWe've run out of petrol.
look up tosaygı duymak, örnek almakI've always looked up to my father.
look down onküçümsemekDon't look down on people.
catch up withyetişmekWalk slower so I can catch up with you.
get away with(cezasız) paçayı kurtarmakHe cheated and got away with it.
cut down onazaltmakI'm trying to cut down on sugar.
keep up withayak uydurmakIt's hard to keep up with technology.
go through with(zor bir şeyi) sonuna kadar götürmekShe went through with the operation.
Türkçeden farkı: Üç parçalı öbek fiillerin anlamı çoğu zaman kelimelerden tahmin edilemez: "put up with" = katlanmak. Bunları, Türkçe karşılığı ve bir örnek cümleyle birlikte kalıp olarak öğrenmek en etkili yoldur. Son kelime edat olduğu için arkasından fiil gelirse -ing olur: "look forward to hearing".

Kendini dene

I can't ___ his rude behaviour any longer.

Seçenekler: put up with / get on with / look up to

Cevap: put up with. Katlanmak.

You should cut down ___ coffee.

Seçenekler: on / with / of

Cevap: on. cut down on.

Do you ___ your neighbours?

Seçenekler: get on with / put up with / run out of

Cevap: get on with. İyi geçinmek.

The thief ___ stealing the car.

Seçenekler: got away with / came up with / looked up to

Cevap: got away with. Paçayı kurtarmak.

I missed a week of school, so I need to ___ the others.

Seçenekler: catch up with / look down on / cut down on

Cevap: catch up with. Yetişmek.
