İleri isim cümlecikleri

B2'de that, wh- ve whether ile isim cümleciklerini gördük. C2'de bu cümleciklerin daha karmaşık ve üslupsal kullanımlarına bakıyoruz.

YapıÖrnek
özne olarak thatThat the plan failed is hardly surprising.
özne olarak whetherWhether he will agree remains to be seen.
-ever ileWhoever wins will receive a prize. Whatever you decide is fine with me.
the fact that / the idea thatThe fact that he lied is unforgivable.
isim + that (içerik)the belief that, the assumption that, the notion that
sıfat + that / wh-It is doubtful whether... / I'm not sure what...
edat + wh-It depends on who you ask. / We talked about what to do.

remains to be seen ve benzeri

Resmî yazıda belirsizliği ifade eden kalıplar: It remains to be seen whether... (... olup olmayacağı henüz belli değil), It is open to question whether..., There is no telling what..., What matters is whether...

Türkçeden farkı: Türkçede isim cümlecikleri "-dığı, -ip -mediği" ekleriyle kolayca özne olabilir: "Planın başarısız olması şaşırtıcı değil." İngilizcede "That the plan failed is not surprising" yapısı mümkün ama resmîdir; çoğu zaman "It is not surprising that the plan failed" daha doğaldır. "Whether ... remains to be seen" kalıbı akademik ve haber dilinde çok yaygındır.

Sık yapılan hatalar

YanlışDoğruNeden
If he will come is unknown.Whether he will come is unknown.Özne: whether.
Who wins will get a prize.Whoever wins will get a prize.-ever gerekir.
It depends on who do you ask.It depends on who you ask.Olumlu sıra.

Kendini dene

___ he will accept the offer remains unclear.

Seçenekler: Whether / If / That if

Cevap: Whether. Özne: whether.

There is a widespread ___ that technology makes us happier.

Seçenekler: assumption / assuming / assume

Cevap: assumption. isim + that.

___ you say will be used against you.

Seçenekler: Whatever / However / That

Cevap: Whatever. Whatever.

It remains to be ___ whether the company will survive.

Seçenekler: seen / seeing / see

Cevap: seen. remains to be seen.

___ she was innocent became clear later.

Seçenekler: That / What / If

Cevap: That. That + cümle.
