Verb + -ing or to + infinitive

Fiil + -ing / to · A2 Temel seviye İngilizce dilbilgisi

Fiil + -ing

Bazı fiillerden sonra başka bir fiil gelecekse -ing biçiminde olur. Bunları kalıp olarak öğrenmek en iyisidir.

Fiil + -ingÖrnek
enjoy, like, love, hateI enjoy cooking.
finish, stopHave you finished eating?
mind, don't mindDo you mind waiting?
keepHe keeps calling me.
avoid, suggest, practiseShe suggested going by train.
go + aktivitego swimming, go shopping, go skiing

Fiil + to + fiil

Fiil + toÖrnek
want, would likeI want to travel.
decide, planWe decided to stay.
hope, expectI hope to see you soon.
need, tryYou need to rest.
learn, promise, agree, refuseShe learned to drive.
forget, offer, seemDon't forget to call.

Bazı fiiller ikisiyle de kullanılır ve anlam pek değişmez: like, love, hate, start, begin, continue. It started raining. = It started to rain.

Türkçeden farkı: Türkçede bu fark yok: "Yüzmeyi severim", "Yüzmek istiyorum" ikisinde de fiil isimleşir. İngilizcede hangi fiilden sonra -ing, hangisinden sonra to geldiği ezberlenmelidir. Bir ipucu: want, decide, hope, plan gibi geleceğe yönelik fiiller genellikle to alır.

Sık yapılan hatalar

YanlışDoğruNeden
I enjoy to read.I enjoy reading.enjoy + -ing.
I want going home.I want to go home.want + to.
She suggested to go.She suggested going.suggest + -ing.

Kendini dene

We went ___ last weekend.

Seçenekler: to shop / shopping / shop

Cevap: shopping. go + -ing.

Do you mind ___ the door?

Seçenekler: to close / closing / close

Cevap: closing. mind + -ing.

He stopped ___ two years ago.

Seçenekler: to smoke / smoking

Cevap: smoking. Bıraktı: stopped smoking.

I hope ___ you again.

Seçenekler: seeing / to see

Cevap: to see. hope + to.

They kept ___ during the film.

Seçenekler: to talk / talking

Cevap: talking. keep + -ing.
