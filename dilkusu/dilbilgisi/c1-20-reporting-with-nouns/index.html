Reporting with nouns

İsimlerle aktarma · C1 İleri seviye İngilizce dilbilgisi

İsimlerle aktarma

B2'de aktarma fiillerini (claim, deny, suggest) gördük. Resmî ve akademik yazıda bu fiillerin isim biçimleri de aktarma için kullanılır. Bu, metni daha nesnel ve yoğun yapar.

Fiilİsim + kalıpÖrnek
claimthe claim thatThe claim that the drug is safe has been questioned.
suggestthe suggestion thatHis suggestion that we leave early was ignored.
arguethe argument thatThe argument that taxes should rise is weak.
admitan admission that / ofHis admission of guilt surprised everyone.
denya denial of / thatShe issued a denial of the rumours.
promisea promise to / thatThe government's promise to cut taxes
warna warning that / aboutThere was a warning about the storm.
refusea refusal toHer refusal to cooperate
decidea decision toThe decision to close the school

Sık kullanılan isim kalıpları

Haber ve akademik dilde: There is evidence that..., There are fears that..., There is a widespread belief that..., The view that... is common., Despite assurances that..., Following reports that...

Türkçeden farkı: Türkçede "... iddiası, ... önerisi, ... reddi, ... kararı" gibi isimler -ın/-in tamlamasıyla kurulur: "Hükümetin vergileri düşürme sözü". İngilizcede isimden sonra genellikle that + cümle ya da to + fiil gelir: "the government's promise to cut taxes". Haber metinlerini okurken bu yapıyı tanımak anlamayı çok kolaylaştırır.

Kendini dene

The ___ that he stole the money is completely false.

Seçenekler: claim / claiming / claimed

Cevap: claim. İsim: the claim that.

There is growing ___ that the plan will fail.

Seçenekler: concern / concerned / concerning

Cevap: concern. İsim: concern that.

The company issued a ___ of the accusations.

Seçenekler: denial / deny / denying

Cevap: denial. İsim: denial.

There is a widespread ___ that money brings happiness.

Seçenekler: belief / believe / believing

Cevap: belief. belief that.

The minister's promise ___ unemployment has not been kept.

Seçenekler: to reduce / reducing / of reduce

Cevap: to reduce. promise to.
